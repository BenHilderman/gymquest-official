# Lift AI — Positioning Research

Snapshot of the competitive landscape analysis that produced the brand line **"Every post is a workout you can run."** Stored here so future product/design/marketing decisions can be checked against the evidence that led to this positioning.

---

## Brand line (current)

> **Every post is a workout you can run.**

Alternates in the same lane:
- Tap a post. Start the workout.
- Lifts you can actually do.

## The Wedge

### Does anyone own "executable post"?

**No competitor ships this as a first-class primitive.** The closest:

- **Hevy** lets users *save* another user's workout as a routine — but shared routine links **do not include reps and weights** (per Hevy's own help docs). Structure travels, prescription does not. A post is a trophy, not a runnable file.
- **Apple Fitness+ SharePlay** requires everyone be on a FaceTime call at the same time — synchronous, not async-runnable.
- **Strava** gym is unusable ("you can't log what exercises you did, sets, reps, or weight — just 'Workout — 45 minutes'").
- **12REPS** explicitly brands itself "Strava of strength" but delivers coach-authored content, not peer UGC.

### Unmet desires (sourced)

1. **Strava has no home for lifters.** Women's weight-training uploads on Strava grew ~25% YoY — demand spilling over from cardio-social into strength.
2. **Hevy users want richer, proof-based posts.** Top review: *"I wish there was a way to upload short video clips of workout sets so that there would be some somewhat verifiable proof of what people are lifting."*
3. **Community/gym-locality ask.** *"y'all should build gym communities / let people attribute themselves to certain gyms… have a feed for people at that gym."*
4. **Aggressive social features fail.** Consensus on Reddit: "make it optional and easy to ignore." Translation: social must be utility-first ("Try it"), not broadcast-first ("like this post").
5. **Motivation-to-act gap.** JMIR/Reddit study: socializing is ~10% of sustained-exercise factors, tracking only ~2.8%. Current apps are tracking-first; the social delta is largely untapped.

## Positioning Candidates (ranked)

### Option A — **"Every post is a workout you can run."** ← WINNER

- **Who**: 18–28 lifters who already post gym content (IG stories, Hevy logs, TikTok PRs) and have 2–5 training friends.
- **Pain solved**: You see your friend's killer leg day, screenshot it, manually retype 8 exercises into Strong/Hevy. Or a cool program lives as a dead PDF. Lift AI collapses *discover → intent → execution* into one tap.
- **Kills**: Hevy's half-measure sharing (strips prescription); Strong's asocial silo; the Strava-for-lifters gap.
- **Risk**: Network effect — a feed is worthless until your friends are on it. Mitigation: "Try it" links work for non-users (like Hevy's public routine links) so virality is not gated on install.

### Option B — "Train together, on your schedule."

- **Who**: Long-distance friend groups / couples who used to gym together.
- **Pain**: Apple's SharePlay needs real-time FaceTime; nobody does that. You want async "I did what she did, yesterday, in my gym."
- **Kills**: Apple Fitness+ Group Workouts, Fitness Pact, ad-hoc group chats.
- **Risk**: "Together" without liveness is a softer emotional sell than executable utility. Harder to convey in one line.

### Option C — "Strava for lifters."

- **Who**: Strava-native cardio athletes who also lift and want the same feed/kudos for barbell work.
- **Pain**: Strava treats gym as "Workout" blob; Hevy feed is utilitarian.
- **Kills**: Strava's weight-training gap; 12REPS's claim on the same phrase.
- **Risk**: Reference-based positioning ("X for Y") is weak when X's users don't migrate easily, and 12REPS/Motion are already grabbing this phrase. Borrowed identity, not owned.

### Why A wins

Pick A because it's the only positioning that *describes a mechanic no one else has built* rather than borrowing someone else's brand (C) or selling a vibe (B). Review evidence shows users already want richer posts (video proof) and easier ways to adopt others' routines — Hevy half-solved it and explicitly bails on carrying weights/reps through the share. The empty square on the board is "post = executable session," and the product already builds it. Lead with the mechanic; persona marketing comes underneath.

---

## Product Implications

- **Friends, Clubs, Discover, Today are fine** — they're all distribution/discovery surfaces for the core mechanic. Keep.
- **Every new feature should answer**: *does this make it easier for a workout to spread?* If not, cut or defer.
- **The "Try it →" button is the hero** — make it more visible: bigger on cards, more prominent on profiles, logged in telemetry as the north-star metric.
- **Community framing fits cleanly** — friends/clubs are *how* workouts spread. Community is the *distribution mechanism*, not the product itself.
